The Origin

The Making of a Legacy

01 / 10
Chapter 01 2009

A Loss That Became a Launchpad

Bikaner, Rajasthan

At the age of nine, Mehul Purohit lost his father. What followed was not grief that consumed him, but a fire that forged him. With no roadmap and no safety net, a boy in Bikaner decided that adversity would be his raw material, not his ceiling.

Chapter 02 2018

The Spark Before the Storm

The Idea Is Born

Nine years of observation, hunger, and study converged into a singular clarity: the world was moving digital, and brands needed someone who could navigate it fluently. Mehul saw the gap no one else was filling. He decided to fill it.

Chapter 03 2019

First Footprints in the Industry

MYGOV · MPL

Before the agency, there was the craft. Mehul cut his teeth working with MYGOV and MPL, learning the mechanics of digital marketing, influencer ecosystems, and brand communication from the inside. He was studying the machine he would one day run.

Chapter 04 2020

MultiPhase Digital Is Born

February 2020 · Mumbai

In February 2020, with relentless conviction and zero room for failure, Mehul officially founded MultiPhase Digital. The world was heading into a pandemic. He was heading into his life's work. What started as a one-person vision would become Asia's most talked-about PR agency.

Company Founded
Chapter 05 2021

The Big Brands Come Calling

Zee Music · Jio Studios · OTTs

Within one year, MultiPhase Digital was sitting across the table from Zee Music Company, Jio Studios, and India's biggest OTT platforms. The word was spreading: if you wanted PR that actually moved the needle, you called Mehul. He was 20 years old.

Chapter 06 2022

Pan-India. Youngest. Unstoppable.

6 Cities · Asia's Youngest Entrepreneur

MultiPhase Digital expanded to six cities across India: Mumbai, Noida, Bengaluru, Jaipur, Chandigarh, and Surat. The same year, Mehul was recognized as Asia's Youngest Entrepreneur. He had not yet turned 22.

Asia's Youngest Entrepreneur Award
Chapter 07 2023

A Media Platform Is Born

MultiPhase Times · Content at Scale

Recognizing that the future of PR lives at the intersection of media and storytelling, Mehul launched MultiPhase Times, a publishing and thought leadership platform designed to shape narratives at scale. The agency now had its own voice, and that voice would shape public discourse.

Chapter 08 2024

Rooms Most Dream About

International Recognition

2024 saw Mehul photographed alongside Union Minister Nitin Gadkari and celebrated philanthropist-actor Sonu Sood at the Inspiring Leaders Awards. These were not celebrity encounters; they were confirmation that Mehul now occupied rooms where real influence is forged.

International Glory Awards Recognition
Chapter 09 2025

Best Brand Reputation Agency, Asia

International Glory Awards 2025

At the International Glory Awards 2025, MultiPhase Digital was named Best Brand Reputation Management Agency in the presence of Bollywood star Tamannaah Bhatia. The recognition was not for one campaign but for five years of compounding excellence that had reshaped what Indian PR could achieve.

IGA 2025 Winner
Chapter 10 2026

Double IDEA Awards. A New Summit.

IDEA Awards 2026 · 70+ Team

At the Entrepreneur India IDEA Awards 2026, Mehul led MultiPhase Digital to a historic double win: Most Innovative PR Company and Best Digital Campaign of the Year. With 70+ employees, 500+ brands served, and a footprint across Asia, the boy from Bikaner had built one of India's most significant PR empires.
